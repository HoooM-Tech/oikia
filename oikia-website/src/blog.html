PODCASTING / DRAFT EDITORIAL PREVIEW

A good story needs a way
to find its people.

Podcast marketing begins with a question: who is this story for, and where might they be listening?

A thoughtful episode can open a conversation. But first, someone has to discover it. That makes marketing part of how a podcast meets the world, rather than something that happens only after the recording is finished.

Start with the people.

What matters to the audience you have in mind? Which questions are they asking? What would make them stop, listen and return? Those questions can guide how you talk about a show and which parts of its story you choose to share.

Make the invitation clear.

A short clip, a social post or an episode description is an invitation into a longer conversation. It helps when that invitation gives people a clear sense of the perspective they’ll find inside.

There is no single route to every listener. Paying attention to the audience, testing an approach and learning from the response can give the next decision a little more direction.

MEDIA / DRAFT EDITORIAL PREVIEW

Room for more
than one medium.

An idea can begin in one format and find another way to connect.

Some stories begin with a voice. Others begin with a photograph, a sentence or a place. The first form an idea takes does not have to define every possibility it holds.

Let the idea lead.

Before choosing a format, it helps to ask what the story needs. Is its strength in the intimacy of a conversation? The detail of a written reflection? The way a moving image can bring a moment into focus?

Each medium offers a different experience. A story can change as it moves between them, revealing something that was less visible before.

Keep the connection in view.

Exploring a new format is a creative decision, but it is also a question about the audience. The important connection is between the idea, the way it is expressed and the people it hopes to reach.

For OIKIA, podcasting is a foundation from which to look toward a wider world of media. That next chapter starts with curiosity about what else a story can become.

COMMUNITY / DRAFT EDITORIAL PREVIEW

An ecosystem is built
between people.

The spaces between individual projects can be places where a creative community grows.

A creator may work on a single show, but the questions around that work are often shared. How do people discover it? Where can a new perspective be heard? Who has already learned something that could help?

Knowledge becomes more useful when it moves.

Sharing a lesson, a question or an approach can give someone else a starting point. Conversations between independent creators and brands can make different kinds of knowledge visible.

Connection is part of the infrastructure.

A creative ecosystem is more than a collection of outputs. It also includes the relationships, learning and opportunities that allow people to continue their work.

OIKIA’s interest in Africa’s podcasting ecosystem comes from paying attention to these connections. POD Creators Africa brings that interest into focus: more people in the conversation, with more possibility to explore.
